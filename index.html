04 A little history

Born from
the family table.

Yousvv began with a grandmother, a well-loved copper pot, and the belief that the best meals should make room for everyone.

Today, our chefs honor those recipes with the best local produce, modern technique, and the same generous spirit. This is Egyptian hospitality with the windows open.
